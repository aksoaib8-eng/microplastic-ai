ABOUT THE PROJECT

Smart Water Quality Monitoring

MicroDetect AI is a conceptual working model that demonstrates how artificial intelligence and image processing can assist with microplastic screening in drinking water.

The system analyzes images of filtered samples and produces estimated particle counts, concentration and confidence information.

⚠️ Important: This website demonstrates an AI concept. The displayed numerical results are simulated and should not be considered laboratory measurements.
